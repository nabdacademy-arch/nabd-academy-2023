Policies & Transparency

Certificate policy

NABD certificates indicate completion of the named educational activity. They are not university degrees, professional licenses, or governmental accreditation unless the certificate itself explicitly states a documented external accreditation.

Research transparency

Research pages must identify whether a project is a protocol, ongoing study, preprint, published article, or conceptual framework. Results are not presented before data collection and analysis are completed.

Privacy

Public certificate verification should expose only the minimum information needed to confirm authenticity. Sensitive student records belong in the authenticated database and should not be committed to a public repository.

Awards and recognition

Recognition is published only when supported by a certificate, official letter, or verifiable institutional correspondence.
