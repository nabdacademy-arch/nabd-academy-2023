# NABD Global Research Network — launch and outreach pack
Date: 2026-11-07
Institution: أكاديمية نبض العلوم الطبية / NABD Academy of Medical Sciences
Founder & General Director: Mohammed Khaled Al-Arja

## Purpose
Establish a supervised, transparent, bilingual medical research-learning network for students in Gaza while requesting exploratory institutional partnerships. This is a proposal, not an existing international partnership.

## Pilot design
1. Charter and research integrity: designate qualified scientific advisor(s); consent, privacy, and credit rules; no collection of real patient data on public forms.
2. Starter project: a literature review or review of the safety/clarity of Arabic-language medical educational resources, scoped by a qualified supervisor.
3. Mentor outreach: request 1–2 hours/month of remote methodology guidance; consider mentors, not guaranteed contributors.
4. Milestone evidence: record confirmed mentors, approved protocols, meetings, completed outputs and letters; publish no unsupported claims.
