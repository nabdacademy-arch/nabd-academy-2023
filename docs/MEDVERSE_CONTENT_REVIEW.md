# NABD MEDVERSE — Clinical Content Release Checklist

**Status:** All cases are *unreviewed educational prototypes*. Nothing in this library should be interpreted as a clinical decision support system, treatment protocol, validated competence measure, or official student exam.

**Release owner:** NABD Academy academic / clinical review board (to be designated).

## Review workflow

For every scenario a named qualified clinical reviewer must sign off:
1. Suitability for its declared educational level and audience.
2. Accuracy and safety of red-flag identification, urgent evaluation, and handover wording.
3. Clinical context and limits of every suggested and distractor answer (0, 1, 2 learning points).
4. Arabic and English clinical terminology checked independently for equivalent meaning.
5. Clinical references reviewed for the **specific** statements in the case, publication dates, jurisdiction, and local clinical practice differences. Current broad links in the pilot do not establish accuracy.
6. Sensitive topics (mental health, paediatrics, pregnancy, poisoning) require specialty review and privacy assessment.
7. Documentation of reviewer name, specialty, date, case revision, change notes, evidence links and final approval.
8. Recheck after every meaningful change to case wording or answer scoring.

## Teaching and learner protections

The displayed heart model is conceptual, not anatomical scale. The points are formative, not validated clinical competencies, and cannot trigger certificates. No real patient data or student-account identifiers are collected. No claims of medical licensing, hospital accreditation, or degree-granting authority should be made. The virtual hospital remains a restricted preview until the full review process and release gates are completed.
